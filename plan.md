## Weaknesses and the steps to fix them

**Calculations.** Evidence: Item 6: wrote '2.4' for a density, with no formula and no unit; mixed g and kg in item 12.

- Formula, Fill, Finish: write the formula, fill in numbers with units, finish with a unit
- Convert to SI units before you start: g to kg, cm³ to m³
- Ten calculations a week, each in three lines
- Course lessons: 3 (Measurement, Errors and Significant Figures), 4 (Density), 6 (Forces, Weight and Derived Units)

**Graphs.** Evidence: Item 9: joined the points dot to dot; axes with no units; gradient from a tiny triangle.

- Label every axis 'quantity / unit'
- One smooth line of best fit, never dot to dot
- Gradient from a big triangle: more than half the line
- Course lessons: 2 (Graphs, Best-Fit Lines and Gradients), 10 (Motion in a Straight Line)

**Electricity.** Evidence: Item 21: said current is 'used up' by the first lamp in a series circuit.

- Series: the same current everywhere; energy is shared, charge is not used up
- Draw the circuit, then put the meter readings on it
- V = IR with units, every time
- Course lessons: 30 (Current, Charge and Conductors), 32 (Circuit Diagrams, Series and Parallel, and Cells), 33 (Resistance, Ohm's Law and Meters)

**Explanations.** Evidence: Item 15: 'the metal is cold' for why a metal tap feels colder than wood.

- Name it, particles, result: name the process, tell the particle story, state the result
- Learn each definition word for word, then say it in a 60-second video
- Course lessons: 16 (Kinetic Theory, Phases and Expansion), 20 (Conduction, Convection and Radiation)

**Experiments.** Evidence: Item 24: changed length and mass together in a pendulum plan.

- Change one, measure one, keep the rest the same
- Every plan: aim, apparatus, method, variables, results table, expected result
- Course lessons: 1 (Galileo, the Scientific Method and the Simple Pendulum), 3 (Measurement, Errors and Significant Figures)

## Habits

- Focus blocks: 25 minutes with the phone in Granny's room, then a 5-minute phone break
- Do Not Disturb from 7 to 9 pm; a 60-minute daily limit on TikTok

**Motivation.** Evidence: Works only when someone checks; no physics homework at all since September.

- A streak calendar on the fridge: one tick for each focus block
- Start with ten minutes; a 60-second physics video only after the work, never instead of it
